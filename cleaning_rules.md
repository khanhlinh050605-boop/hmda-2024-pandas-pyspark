# Cleaning rules (Pandas and PySpark must give equivalent results)

1. Only columns in the allow-list of `src/core/schema.py` are read; `FORBIDDEN_RE` blocks the demographic and post-decision variables listed in the README.
2. The status of numeric variables is determined **field by field** from the public HMDA 2024 documentation/FIG. `income` takes a number or `NA`; therefore `1111` and `1111.0` are both valid income of 1,111 thousand USD. `1111`, `8888` and `9999` are not treated as missing/Exempt on every field. `Exempt` is only a status on fields where the documentation allows it.
3. Values that parse but cannot be used in a log/ratio computation, or fall outside the configured analysis domain, are kept; they get `v_<field>=0` and the band `~OUTSIDE_DOMAIN`. This flag does not claim the reported data is wrong. No zero-filling and no automatic outlier deletion.
4. DTI uses the public HMDA bands exactly: `<20`, `20-<30`, `30-<36`, `36-<43`, `43-<50`, `50-60`, `>60`. Both 50 and 60 belong to `50-60`; only values above 60 belong to `>60`. CLTV and DTI are percentages. LTI = `loan_amount USD / (income thousand USD × 1,000)` and is only computed within the configured positive domain.
5. The denominator of the denial rate is actions 1–3; applications are actions 1–5; originations are action 1. Interest rate is different from rate spread and does not represent the total cost of borrowing.
6. Repeated fingerprints use every permitted public column. The baseline deletes no rows. `is_dup=1` for **all** rows belonging to a fingerprint group of two or more rows; the metadata records `n_dup_rows` (rows in repeated groups) and `n_excess_dup_rows` (excess copies beyond one row per group) separately. The sensitivity `exclude_all_repeated_fingerprint_rows` removes all those rows; this is not a claim that the records are wrong.
7. `has_reported_exempt_value=1` means the record has at least one field that actually carries the `Exempt`/code 1111 status on an allowed categorical field. `lenders_with_exempt_values.csv` is only the set of lenders with at least one such record; it must **not** be called a list of lenders exempt from reporting.
8. `sample_u` is a deterministic key from `part_no` and `row_idx`, giving nested 25%/50%/100% samples. Synthetic data is for testing only; no synthetic number may enter the report.
